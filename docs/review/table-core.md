# Review: the table core (`web3d/src` model, layout, zones, choreography, counting, moments, cardinfo, a11y, ui, main, engine; kit `lit` hook and `eyes` option)

Reviewed against `docs/TABLE3D.md` 0-3 and 5.2, `docs/PROTOCOL.md` and
`docs/RULES.md`, on `9959424` plus the uncommitted `zones.js`, `bin/gate`,
`strohmandeln.html` and `web3d/test/eye.test.js`. The reviewer did not write the
code. Nothing was run in a browser; claims below come from reading the code, the
kit director (`kit/js/director.js`) and the strings.

## What was checked and is right

- `model.reduce` against the PROTOCOL event list: every card-moving event is
  handled; the opponent's hand is backs, with codes only from pulls and
  announcements; the model's `opp`, `packets`, `hidden`, `covers`, `won` and
  `counted` agree with `check()` in `model.test.js` over three whole sittings.
- No face leak found: `layout` gives a code only to the human's hand, covers,
  the held card, the trick and the count; the opponent's known cards are backs
  (`known`), hidden packet cards and the pack are null. `choreography.test.js`
  checks placements and `reveal`s against `engine.audit()` at every reply. The
  cut shows two cards of the human's own hand (`choreography.js:324`), not
  secret.
- The page holds no follow-suit or trump logic: `lit` is `state.legal`, a
  refusal is a key, the announce prompt uses `legal` announce entries. The
  helpers `suitOf`, `isTarock`, `rankOf` exist but are unused (item 20).
- Kit: `eyes` and the portrait `reach` flow through `scene.js`, `framing.js` and
  `aim` consistently, with Cassino's eyes as the default; `defineGame.lit`
  defaults to the old floor (but see item 12).

## Notes on items not raised

- `askRefusal` sends an unlit card on purpose (spec 3.2); if the engine accepts
  it, the card is played and the page advances: consistent.
- The model cannot drift from the wasm state on the paths tested (`fold` equals
  `fromState` equals `check`); drift can only come from items 9 and 24. The
  console `check` in `restore` only logs.
- `persist` after every reply writes `engine.export()` including the seed: the
  record gives every future shuffle (engine-core review item 15, still open).
