## Findings

Severity: bug, should, nit. Ordered by severity.

### Should

1. **The wasm-to-native ratio is not reproduced** (`docs/OPPONENT.md:99-103`, "1.8 times the native time (median 1.8, p95 2.3, over 1 461 identical hints)"). Three runs of the commands given, 2 sittings each, ~270 hints over 1 ms: wasm/native median 0.54-0.55, p95 1.3-1.8 (wasm faster). The machine was loaded (load average 4 on 2 vCPU, other sessions building), so neither figure is clean, but the sentence "times 1.8 in the browser" is stated as measured. Fix: re-measure on an idle machine and give the range, or drop the multiplier and say "same order as native; Node 18, loaded VM"; do not project to the browser from it.
2. **The whole hint after the change is not stated** (`docs/OPPONENT.md:93`, "The whole level-4 hint was 4.6 ms median, 13.6 ms p95, 35 ms max (leads)"). "Was" is the before figure, and it is below the new odds row alone (12 / 49 / 94 ms), which reads as a contradiction. The number that matters for a main-thread call is the after: by the document's own deltas it is about 17 ms median, 60+ ms p95, ~120 ms max natively (my small run: level 4 lead 16 / 34 / 37 ms, follow 15 / 27 / 28 ms, 20 each). Fix: add the after row (`hintbench` table 3 already prints it) next to the before.
3. **`docs/MESSAGES.md` does not know the grouping** (`MESSAGES.md:42-50`, "Hint reasons": "the rest are counts"). The rule that the table groups a count of 1,000 or more by language (1,000 / 1.000) is a table rule on a message parameter and belongs there; `docs/PROTOCOL.md` only had its example changed (fine). Fix: one sentence in "Hint reasons" (parameters), and say `lead-rate`/`lead-cheap` `worlds` is 1000 and `odds.worlds` at most 50 (can be fewer when a search does not finish).
4. **`PLAN.md` open items now stale** (orchestrator's file, not edited here): line 70 "hint latency in wasm unmeasured (native ~125 ms)" and "the odds line's mean can come from a few large deals" are touched by this work (wasm measured with finding 1's caveat; mean unchanged); `docs/review/hint-reasons-engine.md` finding 7 quotes 125 ms for the old 10-world hint.

### Nits

5. **`count` groups fractions** (`web3d/src/strings.js:58`). `count(1234.5678)` gives `"1,234.5,678"`. The comment says "a whole count" and nothing passes a fraction, so not reachable; use `Number.isInteger` or format with `Intl.NumberFormat` per language (`en`/`de`) instead of the regex, which also drops the language branch.
6. **`hintParams` and `count` use different thresholds** (`hinttext.js:13`, `v >= 1000` vs `Math.abs(n) < 1000`). Negatives are never routed to `count`, so `count`'s `Math.abs` is dead from this caller. Fix: route every numeric non-`_thirds` param through `count` (`typeof v === "number"`), and let `count` own the threshold. Checked: 0, -0, 999, 1000, -1000, -1234567, 12345 all format correctly in `count`; German swaps the separator only.
7. **`count` has no direct test** (`web3d/test/strings.test.js`). It is only exercised through the hint line. Add: 0, 999, 1000, -1000, 1234567, `de` and `en`, and a language switch via `setLang`.
8. **Spoken text** (`main.js:792`) uses the same `count`, so a screen reader gets "1.000 von 1.000" / "1,000 of 1,000" with the language's separator, which TTS reads as a number in both languages. No change needed; no test asserts the spoken form (`hintSpoken` of a lead-rate line). Add one assertion.
9. **`odds_config` multiplication** (`hint.rs:121`) can overflow `u64` only for a budget near `u64::MAX` (not reachable: `Config::default` is 30 000, `tournament` takes small flags). `saturating_mul` costs nothing. The integer division drops the remainder when `nodes` is not a multiple of `budget.worlds` (7 worlds, 30 000 nodes: 4 285 per world), which is the same share `move_table` itself uses. `budget.worlds == 0` is guarded by `.max(1)`. Default gives 3 000 per world, 150 000 nodes for 50 worlds: sane for the top rung.
10. **`hintbench` prints `-0.0%`** (noise table, `bin/hintbench.rs` ~line 360): the summed binomial tail exceeds 1 by rounding, so `low * (1 - low)` is a tiny negative. Clamp with `.max(0.0)`. Its header comment refers to `docs/OPPONENT.md` "The tutor", which is not a heading; the section is "Hint evidence".
11. **Redundant pieces.** The ignored test `hint_latency` (`hint.rs:465`) is superseded by `hintbench` table 3 and quotes the old figure in its history; remove it, or keep one. The native example + Node script (`crates/strohmandeln-wasm/{examples,tests}/hintbench.*`, 100 lines) are worth keeping only for the wasm comparison finding 1 questions; once the ratio is re-measured they are the reproduction of a claim the document makes (CLAUDE.md: a computed number a document relies on has a check), so keep them, and say in the document what they do not show (a phone). `bin/hintbench.rs` (400 lines) is large for a one-off but is the reproduction of every other table; keep. Prose tense in `OPPONENT.md` ("before", "(now)", "was") dates quickly; prefer a dated record: "measured 15 Oct 2026 with 24 / 10, changed to 1 000 / 50".
12. **Environment, not the branch.** With the shared `CARGO_TARGET_DIR=/home/andrewbartnof/strohmandeln/target`, `cargo clippy --all-targets` fails with "no `odds_with` in `hint`" (a stale `strohmandeln-ai` artifact from another worktree is reused); `cargo run`/`test` are unaffected, and clippy passes with a private target directory. Worth knowing for parallel worktrees.

## Checked and fine

- **The hinted move cannot change.** `hint()` computes the action by `Opponent::decide` first; the lead sample (stream `0x4813`) and the odds search (`0x4812`) are separate streams drawn after, and the scoring stream `0x4811` still uses `rung3::WORLDS` (24). Test `a_lead_s_evidence_is_over_the_hint_s_many_worlds_and_the_move_is_the_opponent_s` compares to a fresh `Opponent::decide` over three walks at level 4 and checks `worlds == 1000` on every lead-rate/cheap line.
- **Opponent and browser seeds untouched.** The diff of `crates/strohmandeln-ai` outside `hint.rs` and the new bin is empty; `rung3::WORLDS`, `rung4`, `opponent.rs`, wasm `lib.rs` are unchanged, so the golden/strength tests and `?level=1` seeds are unaffected.
- **Odds at bids and forced moves.** Bids return earlier (no odds); `k.legal.len() > 1` guards forced plays; `Extend` returns `decline-extension`; if no world finishes, `move_table` returns an empty `per_world` and `odds_with` returns `None`. Unscaled, 50 worlds in 30 000 nodes gives odds in 22% of positions against 45% scaled (my small run), which supports the scaling.
- **`odds_with`** is `odds_for` with the world count as a parameter: no behaviour change at the same count (commit 4193bbf is a pure refactor and says so).
- **Count formatting:** 999 stays `999`; 1 000 is `1,000` (en) / `1.000` (de); in a card name `romanName` is unaffected; `count` omitted by a caller gives the plain number (tested). Tests cover both languages and the odds line.
- **Document figures:** the lead-rate rows (24 / 100 / 1 000 / 10 000) and the `lead-cheap` threshold (`wins * 10 <= worlds * 2`, 20%) match `explain.rs`; the binomial helper is unit-tested; the tables' shape reproduces from the command given (small run above agrees with the document's medians for 1 000 leads: 2.0 ms vs 1.9, odds 50 worlds scaled 12.5 vs 12). Full-size numbers (2 300 positions) were not rerun (about 10 minutes on a loaded VM).
- **Page:** `web3d/strohmandeln.html` is rebuilt in its own commit and `kit/build.py --check` passes.

## Verdict

Correct and safe to merge: the hinted move, the opponent and the seeds are unchanged, the odds scaling is sound at the top rung and at bids/forced moves, and the count formatting is right in both languages. Before it is called done: fix 1 and 2 (the document's wasm ratio does not reproduce, and the post-change whole-hint cost is missing), 3 (MESSAGES.md); the nits can follow or be recorded in `PLAN.md`.
