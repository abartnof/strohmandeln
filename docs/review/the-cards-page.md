## Findings

Severity: bug, should, nit. None is a bug.

### Should

1. **"Fortune-telling found them only in 1781" overstates Dummett** (`web3d/tutorial.en.md:35`, `web3d/tutorial.md:37`; the line comes from `research/15-the-pack-today.md` "For the tutor" 1). Dummett reports a negative result of a sustained search: no mention of cartomancy or occult meaning "from before 1781". It is the earliest known mention, not a proved first use; 1781 is de Gébelin's occult reading (Mellet's essay in the same volume speaks of Egyptian fortune-telling), and the first divination method is Etteilla's of 1783 (15-S1 ch. 6, 15-S6). "Only in 1781" states an event; the source gives a floor. Fix (both lines stay under 25 words): EN "These cards were made to play with, in Italy, by 1442. Fortune-telling is first heard of in 1781, in France." DE "Diese Karten wurden zum Spielen gemacht, in Italien, spätestens 1442. Vom Wahrsagen ist erst seit 1781 die Rede, und zwar in Frankreich." Update the regex tests (`1781` still matches) and the TABLE3D row ("fortune-telling is French and later, 1781" is fine as it stands).
2. **"Piatnik of Vienna still prints this pack" is not what 15-S11 shows** (`tutorial.en.md:36`, `tutorial.md:38`). The saved page is a shop page for "Tarockkarten Karo, 1 x 54 Blatt in Faltetui, 63 x 113 mm, Artikelnummer 193613" among other Piatnik tarock packs (Blitz, Ornament, Jugendstil, Kaffeehaus ...). It says nothing about the pattern ("Karo" is the back's name; research 15 itself notes "the page gives count, size and item number, not the pattern") and nothing about Vienna or printing (no "Wien" in the page). The Type C identification rests on the 63 x 113 mm size (08-S9), an inference. Worse for the reader, "this pack" collides with line 2 of the next section, "On a real pack the courts carry pictures, not letters": Piatnik does not print this game's pack. Fix: say what the source shows, EN "and Piatnik still sells Tarock packs." DE "und Piatnik verkauft noch heute Tarockblätter." If "of Vienna" is kept, cite a source that states it (08-S3 on Ferdinand Piatnik is about the Moser shop, not the present firm); otherwise drop it. The research note and the sources header should record that the Vienna/pattern link is inferred.
3. **TABLE3D 6.2 acceptance list is stale** (`docs/TABLE3D.md:1311`): `tutor_pages`: "`packets` at the deal". The first page at the deal is now `the-cards`, then `packets`. Fix: "`the-cards` and `packets` at the deal".
4. **Pacing: the first page is the one the first decision needs least.** TABLE3D 6.2.2's own rule is that a page "says only what that decision needs"; `the-cards` explains no decision, and a newcomer now meets two pop-ups at the first deal and a third at the first bid before anything can be played. Order judged right: `the-pull` and the Large Text faces use "tarock" and the court letters, which only this page defines, and `packets` does not need it, so `the-cards` first is defensible. What is too much is the length of the history: bullets 2 to 4 (Italy 1442, 1781, Piatnik, Tarock/Tarot) are interest, not play. Proposed: keep the page (the maintainer's call) but state the exception in 6.2.2 ("`the-cards` is orientation, not tied to a decision") so the rule and the table agree, and consider moving bullets 2 and 4 behind Help only, leaving at the deal the trumps line, the Cavall and the faces line (3 to 4 bullets). If the maintainer wants the whole page at the deal, the TABLE3D wording should still change.

### Nits

5. **German idiom** (`tutorial.md`). Line 37 "Das Wahrsagen kam erst 1781 dazu, aus Frankreich": correct but clipped; "aus Frankreich" hangs after "dazu". See finding 1 for the replacement. "spätestens 1442" is natural and right (Dummett says "by"). Line 38 "druckt dieses Blatt weiter": "weiter" alone with a transitive verb is thin; see finding 2 ("noch heute"). Line 43 "das Blatt zu 52": elliptical and, in Austrian card talk, "Blatt" also means a hand ("mein Blatt"); the usual term is "das Französische Blatt". Fix: "Das Tarockblatt hat ihn behalten, das Französische Blatt zu 52 Karten hat ihn verloren." (25 words or fewer; update the `Blatt zu 52` regex in `tutorial.test.js`). "Hier wird kein Schicksal gedeutet" and "Wenn ich mir erlauben darf" are in the Kibitzer's formal register, as on the packets page.
6. **English and German differ by a clause** (`tutorial.en.md:37` against `tutorial.md:39`): German ends "daher die Verwechslung", English has no equivalent. Harmless; add "hence the mix-ups" or drop the German clause for parity.
7. **"Fortune-telling" line before "If I may"** (`tutorial.en.md:36`): "Nothing here tells your fortune. If I may: Austrians still play Tarock" is a non-sequitur joined by the Kibitzer's tic. Say it as two bullets or drop "If I may". Taste.
8. **"These big letters and numerals" is only true in part** (`tutorial.en.md:42`, `tutorial.md:44`). On the trumps the numerals are the real pack's; what departs from a real pack is the court letters and the corner index on suit cards. In Classic the suit cards show pips, not numerals, and the index is small (`tarockfaces.js` INDEX_SCALE 0.78). The research note's line says "These big letters are for a quick look". Fix: "These big letters and corner numbers are for a quick look" / "Unsere großen Buchstaben und Eckzahlen ...", or revert to letters only.
9. **"These cards" in bullet 2** points at the cards on screen, a modern Austrian pack. Say "Tarocks were made to play with" / "Tarocke wurden zum Spielen gemacht". Taste.
10. **`research/15-the-pack-today.md` "For the tutor" is now behind the page**: line 5's German still says "3 Points wert" (the page uses "3 Augen"), line 6's wording was changed, and line 3's "trionfi" sentence was dropped. The TABLE3D row sends readers there for "wording". Fix in a research commit: align the six lines with the page, and record finding 2.
11. **TABLE3D.md:825** the inserted clause leaves one very long line in a wrapped paragraph. Rewrap.
12. **The `tutorial.test.js` header test** pins the source ids, not their truth: 15-S11 is listed as "(Piatnik, checked 15 Oct 2026)" which supports a listing, not "prints". Resolved by finding 2.
