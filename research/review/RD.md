## 5. New findings (leads, not put into the notes)

1. Alvensleben 1853 p.577 softens Haas's "Mißgeburt Tatteln" to "Erfindung Tatteln": a quiet editing trace showing the reprint was edited, not merely copied (sources/05/poly/ia_10431553bsb_djvu.txt l.32943ff).
2. The Nestroy song exists in two textual states (1890 Chiavacci/Ganghofer vs PG-DE). Anyone wanting "period voice" should say which; the PG-DE text gives the authentic-sounding "tous les trois" and "Pagat ansag'n" calls.
3. "Zu ebener Erde": Johann's "von halb drei bis um neune" gives a 6.5-hour session, a neat flavour line for a long-session counter.
4. Rosenau's farce characters Herr Dreyer/Solo/Ultimo could be a naming source for AI opponents (the lines "Unnüße Mühe ..." is Dreyer mocking a rival, so not kind; use only the names).

## 6. Verdicts

- **03 period rulebooks: trustworthy** (with the corrected headline dating). Transcriptions and the divergence table survived a cell-by-cell check; the 25-card text identity across Berlin 1800 / Vienna 1805 / Berlin 1810 is verified. Weakest points: the "1839 = 1842 p.121" equation (inference, now labelled), and the 1893 congress, which is one second-hand newspaper item.
- **05c literary banter: usable with care.** Newspaper and feuilleton quotes read by eye are accurate. Needs care for Nestroy (wrong act, edited text, one misattributed play, "Trull" is not necessarily Nestroy's), the claim that no text of Rosenau survives (wrong), and "§ 13" dating. Snippet-only rows are as reliable as the ANNO OCR, which was spot-checked.
- **05d polyglot terms: usable with care, largely trustworthy.** Quotes and translations from Hungarian, Czech and Polish checked and faithful; overstatements fixed (1756 etymology, Polish 1821 as independent evidence, Czech "full sow", "konterka"). Rosenau 1821 is real.
