## Verdict on design A

Design A (an open "Abend" ended by announced Touren, slate, kreuzer settlement) stands as a reconstruction of multi-hand Tarock practice, but with four caveats: (1) no source applies the Touren rule, the Conto or the money to the two-hand game; (2) "1 Tour = 2 deals" is inference; (3) the ~15-deal sitting figure is not Strohmandl evidence; (4) the Ehrentour trigger in design C was wrong (fixed).

## Answers to the maintainer's questions (a)-(e)

(a) Deals per Tour with two players. Not stated anywhere for two players. Bermann p. 43-44: Touren "enden nach drei- oder sechsmaligem Herumspielen damit, daß dessen Nachbar zur Linken gibt"; Ulmann p. 19: "Bei Beginn der Touren hat jeder Partner nur noch drei-, respective sechsmal Karten zu geben". Each player deals once per Tour. Werner p. 129: in Strohmanndl "wechselt das Geben bei jedem nächsten Spiele ab", and Ulmann p. 70 after a pass "die Vorhand hat die nächste Donne" (the other player). So 1 Tour = 2 deals, 3 Touren = 6, 6 Touren = 12, is a sound inference, not a quotation. Mayr & Sedlaczek (sources/07d/ms_web_2002.html.txt lines 158, 276) say "die nächste Runde, bestehend aus 2 Spielen": a modern term, used only for the doubling, not for ending a sitting. It supports "2 deals = one cycle" but is not period.

(b) Who calls, and when. Bermann p. 43: "Ist beim Beginne des Spieles nicht eine bestimmte Zeitdauer desselben festgesetzt" (a duration agreed at the start, in time, not Touren) the Touren are announced when one wants to stop; "Gewöhnlich überläßt man dies aus Courtoisie dem im stärksten Verlust befindlichen Spieler und nur, wenn dieser es nicht thut, kündigt ein anderer Theilnehmer die Touren ... an." Ulmann p. 19: "ehe die Spielgesellschaft auseinandergeht ... Gewöhnlich geschieht diese Meldung seitens des Verlustträgers." So: at any time during the sitting, by custom the biggest loser. Not "at the start". Both are in the general rules of the modern Tarok games; nothing addresses two players, but with two there is only one possible loser, so the rule transfers trivially (inference). Extension needs everyone's consent (Bermann p. 44).

(c) Price and currency. Werner 1891 p. 61-62 uses "kr." and "fl." and half a kreuzer per Point, but only as a hypothetical ("verabredet worden wäre") for a four-hand Rufer example. Bermann's 1894 book advertises prices in both "80 kr (1 Krone 60 Heller)" (line 6680 of ocr.txt), so both systems coexisted in print by 1894. The 2-3 kr (Strauss, 1894) and 5 kr (1896) figures are for Tarock, not Strohmandl, and are snippet-level. For a game set about 1891: fl./kr., Point at 1/2 to 5 kr. The "Krone from 1892, Gulden = 2 Kronen, sole currency 1900" statements in the note are unsourced here (my own knowledge agrees but nothing saved supports it): mark as unsourced.

(d) 35:35. Werner p. 130-131 (simple game, both reach 35): "unentschieden ... die nächste Partie, sei sie aufgenommen oder nicht, sammt allen Ansagen doppelt zählt": the next DEAL doubles. Mayr & Sedlaczek: the next Runde of 2 deals doubles (and the Nullrunde). Ulmann p. 78: the game is "unentschieden und zählt nicht" (no doubling). Bermann p. 146: "ganz resultatlos oder man bewerthet nach Uebereinkommen die folgenden höher" (the following games, by agreement). Under A with Werner rules: next deal. Offer M&S's two-deal version as a modern option.

(e) End of sitting and carried doubling or account. No period rule found in Werner, Ulmann or Bermann on a doubling still pending when the Touren run out, nor on an account that ends level. Ehrentour is only for "all passed on the last deal". The Block-Tarok "Brunnen" (Ulmann p. 45-47) is the only end-of-sitting mechanism, and it is for 3-4 players and chips: debts incurred after the Touren are pooled and divided. Design must choose; flag as invention.

## New findings (not put into the note)

1. Bermann pp. 143-146 (Strohmann-Tarok): "wird in zweifacher Weise gespielt. Bei der einen wird jede ausgegebene Partie um einen vereinbarten Preis gespielt" - every dealt hand is played out at an agreed price, no passing; the other form has the "Aufnehmen" declaration. In the first form "gewinnt ... der mehr in seinen Stichen Zählende"; 35:35 is remis. Bermann also says the Pagat ultimo cannot be announced, and a lost ultimo counts double. This is a rule-menu divergence for notes 01/02.
2. Ulmann p. 78: "In manchen Kreisen ist es üblich, auch wenn beide Partner passen, das Spiel durchzuführen" (simple game worth 1 Point, draw does not count). Werner p. 130: simple game worth 2.
3. Bermann p. 144: bookkeeping advice to count "wie oftmal das Spiel aufgenommen wurde" - for the Strohmann the equivalent is counting taken-up deals; not sitting-related.
4. Bermann also uses "Tour" for a dealing pass (line 1651 "je 8 in zwei Touren") and Ulmann p. 75 note for "Tour" = one cycle of deals in games en trois; so "Tour" has two senses in these books.

## Verdict

Note 13: usable with care. The book evidence for Touren, Conto, Block-Tarok and Brunnen is accurate; the weak points are application to two players, the Znaim statistic, the Ehrentour trigger in design C, and Bermann page references.
