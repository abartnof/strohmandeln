## Rows checked and found correct

1 (pack, Dummett), 2 (W p. 129 lot then alternate; U p. 70 lot; F "random, players take turns"; Löw winner deals per D; Beck per WP; note it counts U's "nächste Donne" as alternation, fair), 3, 5, 6 (Löw alone; W/U/Be silent rather than opposed, MS "nicht zweckmäßig"), 8 (U p. 69 packets laid after a bid; Be mode 2; MS deals both at once; Beck via WP after "play"), 9, 11, 12 (free choice explicit only in Be p. 145 "steht die Wahl ... frei"; W, U, MS are silent on hand vs packet when both can follow, MS's wording could be read hand-first: minor), 13, 14 (Löw: bidder leads, per D; Furr likewise; others Vorhand), 15-17 (U writes "36 (35 2/3)"; Prager Tagblatt 1892 is in note 03, not re-checked here), 18 (W p. 131; U "zählt nicht"; Be "resultatlos oder ... nach Uebereinkommen"; D and MS "next round"; Beck per WP), 20 (W p. 133, U p. 71, Be p. 146 announce; D; Beck per WP; MS "in den Stichen, still"; Furr none), 22, 23, 24.
Also noted: Bermann's text says 36 cards in 6 throws of 5 (should be 30), a slip in the original; W's example and U give the right number. Bermann's tarock/king "kann ... genommen werden" is permissive wording, though he calls taking up "eine Pflicht".

## Quote match

All 13 German quotations in note 14 were found in the sources (W 3, U 4, Be 2, plus "Vorhand hat selbstverständlich ...", "36 (35 2/3)", "muß ... 4 Points dem Gegner bezahlen") after OCR normalisation. One misattribution (the Valat phrase to Bermann), one ellipsis that hides a clause (Be, rule 10). Translations are faithful.

## Verdict

Usable with care, after the fixes above. The canonical ruleset stands. The three settings stand (both pass, values, Pagat value): each split is shown in both eras from the primary texts and Dummett/WP. Weak points: rule 10's tie rests on reading Ulmann and Bermann as covering the last card; Valat may be a fourth persistent split depending on Beck's dash; the Furr dependence is unproven (no effect on results).
